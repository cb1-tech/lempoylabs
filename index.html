✦ The studio

Small by design.
Open by nature.

Lempoy Labs is an independent studio based in Tokyo, Japan—a place for turning ideas into small digital projects, creative experiments, and new ventures.

We build thoughtful software, creative projects, and small ventures—usually starting with a simple “why not?”

Some ideas become useful tools. Some become stories, shops, or experiments. The thread between them is curiosity, care, and the freedom to try.
